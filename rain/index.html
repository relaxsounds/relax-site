Use rain sound without distractions

Many people like steady ambient sound because it creates a consistent background while they sleep, read, work or study. RELAX keeps the experience simple: choose a scene, choose a rain variation, set the volume and leave it playing for as long as you want.

There are no accounts to create and no complicated controls. If rain is not the right mood, the main RELAX experience also includes city, forest and ocean ambience.

How to use RELAX rain sounds

  1. Open the RELAX experience and choose RAIN.
  2. Select Heavy, Thunder, Sea or Window from the rain variants.
  3. Press play and adjust the volume to a comfortable level.
